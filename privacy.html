Kitchen MEP — kitchen & inventory management. Last updated: 5 September 2026.

This is a starter policy for an internal restaurant-operations tool. Have it reviewed for your jurisdiction before relying on it publicly.

What this app is

Kitchen MEP is an operational tool used by restaurant, food-truck and bakery staff to track mise-en-place production, inventory, recipes, orders and reports. It is intended for use by a business and its staff, not for the general public.

Data we handle

  • Operational data — products, recipes, inventory levels, supplier orders, production scans, staff names and roles, and reports you create.
  • Device preferences — theme, language and settings stored locally in your browser (localStorage). These stay on your device.
  • Access PIN — used to authorize administrative changes; it is exchanged for a short-lived signed token kept only in your browser session.

Where data is stored

Operational data is stored in the business's own backend (Google Sheets / Google Apps Script and, during migration, a Supabase Postgres database). Access is controlled by the business. Public read access is limited to non-sensitive operational data required for the app to function; write access requires the PIN token.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not collect payment-card or financial-account information in this app.
  • We do not track you across other websites.

Staff information

Staff names and roles are stored so the app can attribute production and deductions. If you are a staff member and want your information corrected or removed, contact the business that operates this installation.

Cookies & local storage

The app uses browser local storage for preferences and a session token. It does not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy should go to the business operating this Kitchen MEP installation.
